Services

What I can
help with.

Every project is different, so I shape the work around your data and your question. I am with you from the first conversation to the final result, working alongside you the whole way.

01

Method selection

Choosing the right method is often the hardest part. I will help you find approaches that fit your data and your design, and I explain why they hold up.

02

Data analysis & modelling

From descriptive statistics to regression, network analysis, clustering and latent variable models, I can run the analysis or guide you through it. Either way, it is transparent and reproducible.

03

Study & survey design

A strong analysis starts with a strong design. Let's plan your study, sample size and data collection together, before anything is gathered.

04

Results interpretation

A model's output isn't a conclusion. I'll help you understand what your results mean in context, and just as importantly, why they don't.

05

Methodology review

Already have an analysis? I'll review your statistical choices, point out any weak spots and suggest way to strengthen them.

06

Teaching & tutoring

Statistics is rarely hard because you're bad at it, but because it was explained badly. Exam next week or thesis chapter you keep putting off, we'll start from the idea and work through it at your pace, in line with your course.

How it works

From first message
to final results.

A clear process, so you always know where we are and what comes next.

  1. 01

    You get in touch

    Send me a few lines about your project: your data, your research question, and where you are stuck. You don't need statistical terms, just describe what you are trying to find out.

  2. 02

    We frame the problem

    Together we work out what you are actually trying to answer. Once the question is clear, I send you a timeline and a quote, so you know what to expect before we begin.

  3. 03

    I propose an approach

    I outline the methods I would recommend, explain why, and talk through the trade-offs. There is rarely one right answer, so I tell you what I would do and explain my reasoning.

  4. 04

    Analysis and delivery

    I run the analysis or guide you through it, whichever you prefer. You get results along with explanations.

  5. 05

    Review and refinement

    Research rarely moves in a straight line. If the results open new questions, we go back and refine the analysis. We finish when you understand your results and can stand behind them.

About me

My work sits at the intersection
of methodology and the research it serves.

I am a statistical consultant specialised in academic and applied research sitting at the intersection of quantitative methodology and applications. In particular, my own research focuses on network analysis, Bayesian inference and latent variable models, and my consultancy work is shaped by the question each client brings.

I've collaborated with researchers in statistics, criminology economy and social science, turning real data and important questions into clear analyses that can be understood, defended and explained. What matters to me is choosing models that can be justified, being explicit about the assumptions, and honest about the limits.

You'll work with me directly, from our first conversation to the final result.
